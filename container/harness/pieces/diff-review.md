**Have the change reviewed.** When a change is complete and its checks pass, launch `checker` on the whole change with a self-contained brief: what changed and why (file:line or the diff), what to verify, what is out of scope, and the results of the checks you ran. Fold each defect it asserts (through a doer when this mode uses doers, except small mechanical edits), then launch it again on the whole revised change, telling it to look first at the previous round's fixes, where the next defect usually is. Any change to code makes the review stale: code is anything that runs or is read as behaviour, including configuration, tests, and text an agent is given to follow. Comments, formatting, commit messages and notes do not. The work is done only when the last review saw the final code, and it is committed only after that review.

**Have the change reviewed.** When a change is complete and its checks pass, launch `checker` on the change (each unit's change, when this mode implements in units) with a self-contained brief: what changed and why (file:line or the diff), what to verify, what is out of scope, and the results of the checks you ran. Fix what it finds within the review limits (below) (through a doer when this mode uses doers, except small mechanical edits); for the check of the fixes, launch it again with the fixes' diff and the findings they answer, and say that it is a re-check. Code is anything that runs or is read as behaviour, including configuration, tests, and text an agent is given to follow; comments, formatting, commit messages and notes are not, and a change to them needs no review. Commit only after the last review the limits call for.
